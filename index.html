About me

I'm Carolina Olguin, a Senior QA Engineer with over 17 years of experience in the software industry. Throughout my career I've worked in agile teams, validating everything from web applications to mobile apps — always keeping quality as a business enabler, never a bottleneck.

My strength lies in designing testing strategies that blend intelligent automation with deep technical exploration. I believe the best QA engineers don't just find bugs — they understand the system, anticipate risk, and collaborate to make the product better from the start.

I'm continuously sharpening my skills in Cypress automation, BDD, and REST API testing, with a close eye on the tools redefining what modern QA engineering looks like.
